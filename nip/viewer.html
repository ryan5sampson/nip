Shortcuts

Watch

Space
Play or pause the edit (cuts are skipped)
←→
Back or forward 2 seconds
⇧←→
Jump to the previous or next clip, into the next or previous video too

Mark footage

C
Tap: cut the whole clip under the playhead. Hold: plays, and cuts what plays until you let go.
K
Keep (green), the same way: tap for the whole clip (again to unmark), hold for what plays
⇧C
Cut from the start of this clip (the last split or edge) up to the playhead
⇧K
Keep from the start of this clip up to the playhead
M ←→
Merge this clip with the one on that side, undoing a NIP between them. Anything cut between comes back.
S
Split the clip at the playhead into two, neither cut nor kept; on a split, removes it. Saying “NIP split” or just “NIP” while filming splits too.

Undo

⌘Z
Undo your last change to this video: cuts, keeps, splits, names, takes, applied notes
⇧⌘Z
Redo it

Takes

IO
Set the in or out of the take at the playhead
T
Keep or drop that take

Name clips

N
Name from the start of this clip up to the playhead, as a new clip; ↵ saves it and marks it keep, Esc closes
⇧N
Name the whole clip under the playhead

NIP

E
Tell NIP what to change at this moment, e.g. “cut the stumble”; ↵ adds it, Esc leaves
⇧E
A note on the whole video, e.g. “tighten every pause”
New edit
In NIP’s box, describe a video and NIP builds it from the transcript
Apply
Notes wait until you press Apply, or tick Apply now

Mouse

Timeline
Click to jump there. Click a clip to name, keep or cut it; click a red cut to open it. Drag a clip’s ends to trim it, or out into the cut beside it to extend it.
Transcript
Click a word to jump to it. Click a clip’s heading or edge to select the whole clip, drag over words, or click a silence, then K keep, C cut or N name it. K brings cut text back.
