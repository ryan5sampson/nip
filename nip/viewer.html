Mark footage

C
Cut: press at the start, play or skip to the end, press again
G
Keep (green): same, press at the start and the end
⇧C
Cut the whole stretch under the playhead
⇧G
Keep the whole stretch; again to unmark your keep
S
Split the clip at the playhead into two, neither cut nor kept; on a split, removes it. Saying “NIP split” or just “NIP” while filming splits too.
